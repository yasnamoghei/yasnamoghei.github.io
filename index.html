About

Who I am

I am a screenwriting student at York University and a filmmaker working across scripts, short films and essays. I am drawn to stories that move across borders: the literal ones people cross, and the emotional ones they carry with them long after.

My films tend to start as questions rather than plots. What a person keeps, what they leave behind, and how memory rearranges both. Alongside screenwriting I make short films and cinematic experiments, and I write essays and criticism about the films that shape how I see.

This page collects that work in one place. If something here interests you, I would like to hear from you.
