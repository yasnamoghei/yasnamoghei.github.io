About

Who I am

I'm a screenwriting student at York University, in Toronto. So far that has meant a horror short about a man hunted down a hallway by something wearing his own face, a comedy I made with friends about being too tired to find your car, and a nearly silent film set on the 59th floor of an empty building. I'm not going to pretend they add up to a thesis yet.

I grew up on Iranian cinema and never learned to separate it from everything else, so Mehrjui and Kiarostami sit on the same shelf as Villeneuve and Chaplin, and I will defend that shelf. Mostly I'm interested in what a place does to a person when nobody else is in it, which is probably why so much of what I write happens in corridors, elevators and empty floors.

Alongside the films I write, about movies and around them, and I'm still working out whether that's criticism or just thinking out loud in public. Either way it's here, and I'd rather hear what you make of it than be told it's good.
